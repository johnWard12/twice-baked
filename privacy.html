Twice Baked privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Twice Baked has no ads, no purchases and no accounts. Your progress is saved on your phone.

Cooking together online uses Unity Gaming Services. The game signs in with an anonymous ID (no name, email or phone number), and the two phones connect through Unity's servers, which see each phone's internet address while you play. Your friend's phone gets your chef, the name you set under My Chef, and the progress the two of you share. Cooking on the same Wi-Fi connects the two phones directly.

Unity, the game engine, also collects anonymous device and crash reports. See Unity's privacy policy.

None of this is used for advertising or to track you.

Deleting the game deletes everything it saved on your phone.
